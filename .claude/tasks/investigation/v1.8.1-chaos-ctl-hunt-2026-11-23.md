# Random mid-stream CTL hunt against plain-C libopus (2026-11-23)

- **Objective:** find encoder divergences from plain-C libopus 1.6.1 that the existing one-setting audits miss. The search changes controls, resets and frame sizes in the middle of a stream.
- **Status:** six fixes landed on `fix/v1.8.1-hunt`. Three reproducers are still open (below).
- **Scope:** the single-stream encoder, plus the surround and projection encoders, compared with `enc_oracle --script-enc` and `--script-ms`.

## Harness

The two tests below feed the Go encoder and the plain-C oracle the same random script:
- `libopus_chaos_ctl_opusref_test.go` (`TestChaosEncoderCTLOracle`)
- `libopus_chaos_ms_opusref_test.go` (`TestChaosMultistreamCTLOracle`)

Each script mixes:
- control changes
- `OPUS_RESET_STATE`
- frames of 2.5 to 60 ms
- sine, noise, silence and clipped input
- int16 and float input

The tests compare every packet, final range and control return value.

Settings:
- Defaults: 120 single-stream seeds and 40 multistream seeds.
- `CHAOS_BASE`, `CHAOS_SEEDS` and `CHAOS_FRAMES` widen the single-stream sweep. `CHAOS_MS_BASE` and `CHAOS_MS_SEEDS` widen the multistream sweep.
- `CHAOS_DUMP` logs every single-stream packet, and `CHAOS_HEX` describes a mismatch.
- `TestChaosEncoderCTLMinimize` (with `CHAOS_SEED`) removes controls one at a time while the mismatch persists.

Run it in PowerShell:

```text
go test -count=1 -tags opusref -run '^TestChaos' .
$env:CHAOS_SEEDS='600'; go test -count=1 -tags opusref -run '^TestChaosEncoderCTLOracle$' .
```

`cgoref` links the msys2 SIMD libopus, so its CELT output differs from Go by design. The harness therefore uses the plain-C `enc_oracle.exe` (`pwsh scripts/oracle/build_encoder.ps1`).

## Fixes

| Commit | Divergence |
|---|---|
| `f68a2f3` | A CELT-only packet that followed a delayed stereo-to-mono switch computed its equivalent rate with two stream channels. libopus clears `toMono` first. |
| `be90b10` | `voice_ratio` was not kept through digitally silent frames, and it was cleared on Reset. Silence coded after the analysis was turned off then chose SILK or hybrid. |
| `a254357` | After a Reset, the `silk_mode` outputs (`allowBandwidthSwitch`, `inWBmodeWithoutVariableLP`) reported the reset values instead of the last encode's values. |
| `2a463ae` | With prediction disabled, the previous packet's LBRR was still coded. `silk_Encode` clears the LBRR flags there. |
| `6e54054` | A multistream child stream resolved its bitrate through the 1275-byte frame cap instead of `user_bitrate_to_bitrate`. This is visible at `OPUS_BITRATE_MAX`. |

The single-stream sweep matched in 599 of 600 seeds, and the multistream sweep in 298 of 300.

## Open reproducers

- **Single seed 487** (fs=24000, mono, audio). Minimal controls:
  - `fec=2`
  - `bw=FB`
  - `complexity=0`
  - `loss=50`
  - `fec=0` just before frame 49

  Frame 49 is a 60 ms hybrid packet after 20/40 ms SILK MB packets that carried FEC. Go's first sub-frame is 6 bytes larger, and the next two are about half the size. Suspected cause: the SILK internal rate switch from MB to WB at complexity 0, interacting with the end of FEC.
- **Multistream seed 203** (family 1, 3 channels, 24 kHz, voip). The minimal script is `bitrate=384000` before frame 4. The uncoupled center stream (hybrid SWB 20 ms, about 135 kb/s) is 479 bytes in libopus and 451 bytes in Go. The coupled stream matches. The mismatch predates the 2026-11-22 performance work (it reproduces at `7cc4669`). These match libopus line by line:
  - `surround_rate_allocation`
  - `compute_silk_rate_for_hybrid`
  - the surround SILK masking correction
  - the HB_gain gating
  - the CELT surround mask (skipped in hybrid)

  The next step is to trace the stream's SILK/CELT split against the oracle.
- **Multistream seed 183** (family 255, 3 channels, 24 kHz, voip). The minimal controls are `fec=1`, `vbr=0`, `complexity=5` and `loss=50`. Frame 24 is a 10 ms CELT-to-hybrid switch in CBR, after 2.5 and 5 ms CELT frames. Stream 0 differs at the same size, while the other two mono streams, which get different input, match. This also predates `7cc4669`.

`TestChaosMultistreamCTLMinimize` (`CHAOS_MS_SEED`) reduces a multistream seed, and `CHAOS_DUMP` logs its packets.
